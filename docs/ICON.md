## Prompt final

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final Android app launcher icon for FOOTLAB, based on the attached reference image (reference only). Create one polished square 1024x1024 icon, straight-on, no mockup. Preserve the distinctive fast, curved, italic capital F in vivid lime-to-emerald green, a clearly recognizable black-and-pale football integrated into the lower curve, and a small ascending analytics line with three round nodes on the lower right. Refine and simplify the reference into a crisp premium sports-analytics app icon with bold silhouettes and restrained dimensional gradients, legible when reduced to 48 pixels. The F must read as F, with two horizontal strokes and an open right side. Remove tiny field lines, tiny bars, excessive glow, thin white outlines, and excessive details. Dark evergreen full-bleed background (#061814), very subtle tonal depth. Keep all important logo elements within the central 60 percent of the square, with comfortable dark breathing room on every side, suitable for circular and squircle Android masks; the central mark should remain strong and not appear like a small badge. Background must reach all four edges; do not pre-round the corners, do not add an outer black margin or frame. No wording, no FOOTLAB text, no extra letters, no watermark. Generate a finished, visually balanced and distinctive icon, not a presentation sheet.
